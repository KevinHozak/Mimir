These minimal SVG assets are authored for Mimir and distributed with the repository.

The [First Glow art bible](../../docs/first-glow-art-bible.md) defines the required metadata, license, attribution, naming, export, and source-to-bundle retention fields. The production pipeline must retain this record for every asset and never replace an asset referenced by a historical checkpoint.

The editable source inventory at `assets/world/art-production/assets.json` is part of the provenance record. Each active asset must retain a metadata entry and remain traceable through the Tiled importer into every retained content-addressed bundle. Review artifacts under `.tmp/` are disposable and are not source or license evidence.
