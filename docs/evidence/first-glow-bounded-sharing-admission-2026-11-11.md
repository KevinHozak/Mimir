## Fresh policy and preserved boundaries

Fresh constructors may explicitly select `boundedSharingAdmission: true`, recorded as `bounded-sharing-admission-v1`. It enables no other policy. Default startup/reset and absent-policy histories retain their rules; repeated initialization, initialization against recorded histories and unknown policy versions reject.

Proposed donors are assessed before autonomous selection and again before either participant is paused. Current deficit, fewer than three carried charge, and urgent rest below 70 refuse discretionary sharing. Without an active expedition budget, the existing social care preference also retains its rest priority up to 90 readiness.

For an active recorded initial expedition, pattern-reader preparation or surveyed-shard goal, scratch selectors find the currently selected bounded itinerary. A synchronous witness tests that exact itinerary after subtracting **one gift plus one donor upkeep**. It cannot add recovery actions, source draws or an alternative itinerary to justify the gift. A missing feasible witness or insufficient remaining allowance refuses sharing. Post-probe witnesses retain the originating shared 64-action allowance, actual-rest requirement where applicable and care's 90-readiness departure gate. Initial and pattern goals retain their existing preparation limits. No witness or scratch pin is persisted.

The assessment changes no real resource, goal, reservation or queue. Refused proposals emit objective `sharing-admission` events and proceed through normal selection. They transfer nothing and add no sharing upkeep; other actual actions can still incur their normal costs. Admitted gifts retain the original transfer and both participants' sustenance accounting. Receiving a gift can therefore leave carried charge unchanged, and no benefit is inferred. Source limits, fair waiting-age/ID admission, valid contact owners, actual dispatch/arrival checks, public-artifact gates, known-fact boundaries and retry authorization remain authoritative. Future access remains conditional.
